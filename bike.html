Biking in Door County

Biking in Door County primarily takes place within Peninsula State Park. With 20 miles of diverse cycling options, it includes a mixture of scenic paved roads with almost a constant view of Lake Michigan, that is alongside a mountain bike trail system through the woods. The trail network ranges from beginner friendly to advanced riding for a variety of options depending on comfort level. Most routes have stops to popular locations like the Eagle Bluff Lighthouse and Nicolet Beach. Any route chosen is guaranteed to be filled with great views surrounded by nature.
